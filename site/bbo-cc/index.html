Better BBO Convention Card

The things Bridge Base Online’s convention card manager is missing, added right inside it: Account, then Convention cards, in BBO’s web lobby.

On any card

  • Print that fits. BBO’s Print clips the right-hand column; this prints the whole card across a landscape page.
  • ACBL PDFs. Download the card as the ACBL Classic or the ACBL New convention card, filled in and ready to print or email.
  • Open in a tab. See and edit the card at full size instead of in BBO’s narrow panel.
  • Export JSON. Save a copy of the card in a portable format.
  • Edit on Bridge Craftwork, or Save to Bridge Classroom. Open the card in the card editor here, or in Bridge Classroom, without a file to download and upload again.

On your list of cards

  • Export all your cards, and the cards partners have shared with you, in one JSON file.
  • Import cards from a JSON file, from the card editor’s “Export for BBO” file, or from a PDF: one this extension or the card editor made, or any filled-in ACBL convention card or Bridgodex PDF.
  • A card that differs from one already on BBO is never replaced without asking, and the question says which copy is newer when it can tell. After an import, BBO is told the cards changed, so your partners see the new version.

Private by design

It only runs on BBO’s convention card pages, and only does anything when you click. It talks to BBO with the session BBO’s page already has and never sees your password. A card goes to the card editor or Bridge Classroom only when you click that button. No analytics, no tracking, no accounts.
