Bridge Craftwork DD — privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Bridge Craftwork DD follows the boards you play and look back at on Bridge Base Online, and works out the double-dummy errors on your own device. Nothing leaves your browser unless you ask: DD Details sends one board to be shown in more detail, and DD all asks Bridge Base for an event's boards.

What it does

  • While you play, it analyses each board as it ends and tags it in My Table with the errors by you, your partner and the opponents.
  • When you look back at past events in History, it analyses the boards you open, from what Bridge Base's page has already loaded.
  • If you choose DD all, it asks Bridge Base for the rest of an event's boards itself, the way Bridge Base's own page does: one request a second, without your login.

It can be hidden or shown from the menu on Bridge Base's deal view.

What it reads

Only Bridge Base's own record of your boards: the deal, the auction, the play, the result and the players' Bridge Base usernames, as Bridge Base's page receives them. It does not read chat, your login or password, or anything else on the page or in your browser.

What it keeps

On your device only, and never sent anywhere:

  • the analysis of each board, so going back to an event does not repeat the work (when storage runs short, about 3,500 boards, the oldest go);
  • a tally of errors for each event, for the list of tournaments;
  • a short note of the event you are playing, so its tags survive a reload, discarded after twelve hours;
  • your display settings.

None of this names any player. Removing the extension deletes all of it.

When anything leaves your browser

  • DD Details, when you click it, sends that one board — deal, auction, play and the players' usernames — to a Bridge Classroom page (bridge-classroom.org or bridge-classroom.com), which passes it to the double-dummy viewer at bridge-craftwork.com/bridge-solver. The Bridge Classroom privacy policy covers it from there.
  • DD all, when you choose it, sends requests to Bridge Base, with your username, as Bridge Base's own page does.

Nothing else. No analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared.
